Theory

Prepositions

Express place, movement and duration.

Level 1

Use in with many locations, nach with most cities and countries without articles, and zu for many destinations involving people.

Ich wohne in Berlin.

Level 1 practise →

Level 2

Two-way prepositions take dative for location and accusative for a destination: in der Schule / in die Schule. Movement alone does not imply accusative.

Ich gehe in die Schule.

Level 2 practise →

Level 3

seit describes an ongoing duration, normally with present tense. Some place expressions use contractions: im, am, ins.

Ich wohne hier seit drei Jahren.
