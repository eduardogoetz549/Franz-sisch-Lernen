Word order & questions

Build main clauses, questions and subordinate clauses.

Level 1

In a statement the finite verb is second. A phrase such as Heute counts as the first unit. Yes/no questions begin with the finite verb.

Heute lerne ich Deutsch.

Level 1 practise →

Level 2

A wh-question usually starts with a question word followed by the finite verb. Non-finite verb parts remain at the end.

Wann lernst du Deutsch?

Level 2 practise →

Level 3

weil, dass and obwohl send the finite verb to the end. With a subordinate clause first, the main clause begins with its finite verb.

Ich lerne Deutsch, weil ich in Berlin arbeiten möchte.
