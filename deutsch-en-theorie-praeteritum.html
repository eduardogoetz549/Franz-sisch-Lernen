Präteritum

Describe past states, habits and events.

Level 1

Präteritum is common in written narratives. sein: war, warst, war, waren, wart, waren.

Ich war gestern zu Hause.

haben: hatte, hattest, hatte, hatten, hattet, hatten. Modal verbs are frequently used in Präteritum in speech too.
