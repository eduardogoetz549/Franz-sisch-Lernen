Memory aids & mnemonics

Connect a difficult rule or word with an image, sentence or personal idea.

How it works: Choose something you often forget and invent a short, memorable association.
Saved only on this device.
🗂️

Flashcards

Follow this sequence: New → Unsure → Learned → Confident. Uncertain items return sooner.

✍️

Active recall

Cover the answer, recall it from memory and only then write it down.

1. Read the question.
2. Keep the answer covered.
3. Say or write your answer.
4. Check it.
5. Recall incorrect answers again later.
🗺️

Mindmap

Organise a topic into key ideas and examples. This helps you understand rules and connections.

🔎

Learn from mistakes

After a mistake, ask what the correct answer is and why your answer was incorrect.

1. Identify the mistake.
2. Explain the rule in your own words.
3. Solve a similar question. The app automatically prioritises uncertain answers.
💡

Choose a strategy

Verbs: Person → stem → ending.
Vocabulary: Cover → recall → check.
Pronouns: Find the noun phrase → replace → check position.
Adjectives: Identify the noun → gender, number and case → choose the ending.
